Flightdeck Support

Flightdeck provides cockpit exploration and MCDU practice on Android, iPhone and iPad.

  • Pinch to zoom and drag to pan around the panel.
  • Tap a control to select it, then operate it directly or use its large action button.
  • Choose Learn the panel for explanations; operation is paused while learning.
  • Tap the full-screen button on the panel and use Exit full screen to return.
  • Enable Sound, raise the device volume and check the device’s silent setting if you cannot hear a switch.
  • Additional cockpit areas marked Beta may change and have simulation limitations.

Contact: Choose My Electric. When reporting a problem, include your device model, operating-system version, Flightdeck version and the control involved.

This is an independent study aid. Use the applicable aircraft manual for real-world procedures.

Flightdeck Complete

The app and basic MCDU practice are free. One optional $9.99 USD one-time in-app upgrade unlocks navigation database integration, AOC/datalink, Fly the MCDU, ECAM and failure training. Local prices and applicable taxes are shown by the store before purchase. There is no subscription.

To restore access, open Upgrades / restore purchases and select Restore purchases while using the store account that made the purchase. Android purchases restore through Google Play; iOS purchases restore through Apple. Payment pending or cancelled does not unlock access. If payment completed but access is missing, restore purchases before trying to buy again.

Navigation data

After unlocking, open FMGC / MCDU → Navigation database setup. Download the FAA pack once for offline use. Coverage and cycle are shown before downloading. Source FAA data remains publicly available; the purchase pays for Flightdeck functionality. Do not uninstall the app to resolve a failed download: retry it and retain your existing installation.
